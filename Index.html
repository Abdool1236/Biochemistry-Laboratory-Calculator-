🧪 Important formulas for Biochemistry students

🧪 Solution Chemistry

Molarity:
M = moles ÷ volume (L)

Molality:
m = moles of solute ÷ mass of solvent (kg)

Normality:
N = equivalents ÷ volume (L)

Dilution:
C₁V₁ = C₂V₂

⚗️ Acids, Bases & Buffers

pH:
pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]

pOH:
pOH = −log₁₀[OH⁻]

Henderson–Hasselbalch:
pH = pKa + log₁₀([A⁻]/[HA])

🔬 Spectrophotometry

Beer–Lambert Law:
A = εcl

🧬 Enzymology

Enzyme Activity:
Activity = amount of product ÷ time

Specific Activity:
Specific Activity = enzyme activity ÷ protein amount

Michaelis–Menten:
v = (Vmax[S]) ÷ (Km + [S])

🧬 Molecular Biology

DNA GC Content:
GC% = (G + C) ÷ total bases × 100

DNA Complement:
A ↔ T    G ↔ C

🧪 Concentration

Percentage concentration:
% = (amount of solute ÷ total solution) × 100

🧬 Biomolecules

Biomolecules are organic molecules produced by living organisms that are essential for structure, energy, regulation and life processes.

Major Classes

  • Carbohydrates
  • Proteins
  • Lipids
  • Nucleic acids
  • Vitamins and other biological molecules

Functions

  • Energy production
  • Cellular structure
  • Enzyme catalysis
  • Genetic information storage
  • Cellular signaling

🍞 Carbohydrate Biochemistry

Carbohydrates are biomolecules composed mainly of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen and are important sources of cellular energy.

Classification

  • Monosaccharides
  • Disaccharides
  • Oligosaccharides
  • Polysaccharides

Examples

  • Glucose
  • Fructose
  • Sucrose
  • Lactose
  • Glycogen
  • Starch
  • Cellulose

Important Pathways

  • Glycolysis
  • Gluconeogenesis
  • Glycogenesis
  • Glycogenolysis
  • Pentose phosphate pathway

🥩 Protein Biochemistry

Proteins are polymers of amino acids linked together by peptide bonds. They perform structural, catalytic, transport and regulatory functions.

Levels of Protein Structure

  • Primary structure
  • Secondary structure
  • Tertiary structure
  • Quaternary structure

Functions

  • Enzymatic catalysis
  • Transport
  • Structural support
  • Defense
  • Hormonal regulation

Important Concepts

  • Amino acids
  • Peptide bonds
  • Denaturation
  • Protein folding
  • Isoelectric point

🧈 Lipid Biochemistry

Lipids are hydrophobic or amphipathic biological molecules involved in energy storage, membrane structure and signaling.

Major Lipids

  • Fatty acids
  • Triglycerides
  • Phospholipids
  • Steroids
  • Cholesterol

Important Pathways

  • Fatty acid oxidation
  • Beta oxidation
  • Fatty acid synthesis
  • Ketogenesis
  • Cholesterol metabolism

🧬 Nucleic Acids

Nucleic acids are polymers of nucleotides that store, transmit and express genetic information.

Two Major Types

  • DNA — Deoxyribonucleic acid
  • RNA — Ribonucleic acid

DNA Functions

  • Stores genetic information
  • Replication
  • Transmission of hereditary information

RNA Types

  • mRNA
  • tRNA
  • rRNA
  • miRNA

⚡ Bioenergetics

Bioenergetics is the study of energy transformations in living systems.

Important Concepts

  • ATP
  • ADP
  • Energy coupling
  • Oxidation-reduction reactions
  • Electron transport
ATP is the major energy currency of the cell.

🧪 Enzymology

Enzymology is the study of enzymes, their mechanisms, kinetics, regulation and biological functions.

Important Concepts

  • Enzyme
  • Substrate
  • Active site
  • Enzyme-substrate complex
  • Enzyme inhibition
  • Km
  • Vmax
Michaelis-Menten equation:
v = Vmax[S] ÷ (Km + [S])

Types of Inhibition

  • Competitive inhibition
  • Noncompetitive inhibition
  • Uncompetitive inhibition

🔥 Metabolism

Metabolism is the collection of chemical reactions that occur within cells to maintain life.

Two Major Components

  • Anabolism — building molecules
  • Catabolism — breaking molecules down

Major Metabolic Pathways

  • Glycolysis
  • Krebs cycle
  • Electron transport chain
  • Gluconeogenesis
  • Beta oxidation
  • Urea cycle

🩸 Clinical Biochemistry

Clinical biochemistry applies biochemical principles and laboratory tests to understand health, disease and patient conditions.

Common Laboratory Tests

  • Blood glucose
  • Cholesterol
  • Triglycerides
  • Urea
  • Creatinine
  • ALT
  • AST
  • Total protein
  • Albumin

🧬 Molecular Biology

Molecular biology studies biological processes at the molecular level, especially DNA, RNA and proteins.

Central Dogma

DNA → RNA → Protein

Major Processes

  • DNA replication
  • Transcription
  • Translation
  • Gene regulation
  • Mutation

🛡️ Immunochemistry

Immunochemistry studies the chemical and molecular basis of immune reactions, especially antigen-antibody interactions.

Important Terms

  • Antigen
  • Antibody
  • Antigen-antibody reaction
  • Immunoglobulins
  • Complement system

Applications

  • ELISA
  • Immunoassays
  • Diagnostic testing
  • Research

🧪 Hormones & Signaling

Hormones are chemical messengers that regulate physiological processes throughout the body.

Examples

  • Insulin
  • Glucagon
  • Adrenaline
  • Thyroxine
  • Cortisol
  • Growth hormone

Cell Signaling

  • Receptors
  • Second messengers
  • Signal transduction
  • Protein phosphorylation

💊 Vitamins & Minerals

Vitamins and minerals are essential micronutrients required for normal metabolism and physiological functions.

Fat-Soluble Vitamins

  • Vitamin A
  • Vitamin D
  • Vitamin E
  • Vitamin K

Water-Soluble Vitamins

  • Vitamin B group
  • Vitamin C

Important Minerals

  • Iron
  • Calcium
  • Magnesium
  • Zinc
  • Sodium
  • Potassium

🧫 Membrane Biochemistry

Membrane biochemistry studies the structure, composition and functions of biological membranes.

Major Components

  • Phospholipids
  • Cholesterol
  • Membrane proteins
  • Carbohydrates

Transport Mechanisms

  • Simple diffusion
  • Facilitated diffusion
  • Active transport
  • Endocytosis
  • Exocytosis

🧬 Genetics

Genetics is the study of genes, heredity and variation in living organisms.

Important Concepts

  • Gene
  • Allele
  • Chromosome
  • Genotype
  • Phenotype
  • Mutation
  • Inheritance

Mendelian Genetics

  • Law of segregation
  • Law of independent assortment
  • Dominant traits
  • Recessive traits

🔬 Biotechnology

Biotechnology uses biological systems, organisms or molecules to develop useful products and technologies.

Major Areas

  • Genetic engineering
  • Recombinant DNA technology
  • PCR
  • DNA sequencing
  • Cell culture
  • Fermentation technology
  • Biopharmaceuticals

Applications

  • Medicine
  • Agriculture
  • Food production
  • Environmental biotechnology
  • Research
